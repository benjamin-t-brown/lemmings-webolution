## Original rule and production change

Static REA/Ghidra 12.1.4 used the registered x86 `Lemmings Revolution.exe`, SHA-256 `32675bdb49866e76502713251fdfd2b09078c2481c72ca22ff68ebf4b3737a2f`. The constructor’s event table schedules the two-cell stamp at `0x41011C` before the advance at `0x40FBCC`. The advance raises Y and tests the raised forward foot cell at `0x40FE77..0x40FE7F`. On terrain refusal, `0x40FEE6` turns and `0x40FEFB` attaches Walker before decrement. A separate object-overlap branch can turn and still reach the decrement. The installed receiver bounds are outside these staircase probes. [Retained Builder analysis](../../old/docs/scratch/phase3-round6.md) · [Raw installed grid and receiver bounds](../../build/builder-end-turn-001/installed-grid.tsv) · [REA pseudocode, instructions and xrefs](../../build/builder-end-turn-001/static/rea-evidence.json)

On successful advance, the old count of one goes straight to Shrug at `0x40FF96..0x40FFFF`. With bricks remaining, `0x410032` takes the biased head position and `0x410067` adds facing to its X. `0x42E0F2..0x42E0FC` initializes the upright Y bias to +3 in the original’s Y-up grid. A nonzero forward head cell turns the builder at `0x41009E`. The raw asset values at (114,24) and (114,25) are both `0x1`. The shrug’s animation-complete branch at `0x41ABE3..0x41AC06` attaches Walker with the existing facing. Normal walking then resolves footing, step-up and turns via `0x41BDD4 → 0x41BFFC`, with the tall obstruction turn at `0x41C679`. These instructions establish the branch order and the probe coordinates. The original’s exact movement and timing on these two routes remain unobserved. REA closed without a snapshot and the original game was never run. [REA supporting instructions](../../build/builder-end-turn-001/static/rea-support.json)

`World::step` dispatches `World::stepBuilder`. Its nonfinal head check now samples one column ahead of the advanced foot position using the existing terrain material and wrap rules. The last-brick branch still enters `World::stepShrugger`, whose exit hands off to `World::stepWalker`. The fixed clone turns left at 3767 from (113,27) in attempt 2, then walks to (112,28) at 3782 and (111,29) at 3812. Attempt 4 turns at 3618 from (113,28), then walks to (112,29) at 3646 and (111,30) at 3676. In each case the next column is air with Built support two rows below the turn position, so Walker descends one cell per step. The clone retains its hybrid cadence and material mapping.
