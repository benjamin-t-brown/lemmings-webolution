Playtest 23 N3 reported hard lines between the anti-grav lift quads on **Designed with love** (`M11_KF_1`, node 33). They can look smoother. ## Original drawing

- **Geometry.** The three placed roots are at LVL offsets `0xFBA34`, `0x10243B` and `0x108E42`. Each has two beams and two reflections. Each mesh has 16 kind-7 quads forming two stacked rings of eight. The beam's fade uses diffuse alpha 0, 128 and 255. Reflections use 0 to 128. For example, `0xFBED0` and `0xFC220` share vertices 1 and 2 with matching alpha and slightly different UVs. The clipped and unclipped kind-7 dispatches from `0x46C55D` reach `0x46B350` and `0x46B2A0` respectively. Both submit only when the dot product of the authored normal and local eye minus the first vertex is positive. `0x46D460` packs diffuse ARGB and submits a triangle fan at `0x46D5F3`. ## Clone fix and checks

`runApplicationLeg → scheduleSceneFrame → BackdropScene::renderStage(GadgetEffect) → rasterizeSceneTriangle` is the production path on desktop and in WebAssembly. The effect uses mesh quads. The change is confined to [BackdropScene.cpp](../../src/app/BackdropScene.cpp#L1243).

- Two-sided submission drew the near and far faces of the same lift over each other. Their projected boundaries made abrupt brightness steps. Applying the recovered facing test removed the strongest streaks in the cull-only crop.
- Opaque BMP alpha let the dark rows contribute full alpha between black and lit samples. The shaft now derives texel alpha before bilinear filtering, with zero for black and full alpha for lit texels. On the alpha-capable upload path this produces the original texture and diffuse modulation. The tiled band pattern, linear wrap, Gouraud diffuse and additive blend are retained.
- Strict Less with depth writes let accepted lift fragments, including zero-alpha fragments, suppress later glow. With culling and alpha already corrected, disabling lift depth stores changed 220 pixels in the close frame, around the reflection and pad. Read-only depth is a provisional clone choice. Foreground opaque depth still gates the effect and the completed scene still supplies actor occlusion. The clone draws actors in its later actor pass. Exact lift and actor interaction through the original shared walk remains unverified. The clone audit recorded zero lift depth stores and no same-record double hits at the sampled triangle edges. At yaw 72, foreground depth blocked 1,152 color-changing lift samples.
