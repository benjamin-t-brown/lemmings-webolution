# escape-seams-001 · Tube seams in Escape to Victory

Playtest 19 J1 reported a vertical seam behind the hatch and a horizontal seam around the wood and steel in **Escape to Victory** (`E11_KF_1`, node 28). Both reproduced with scenery disabled. At the settled hatch view (yaw 191/256 turns, eye distance 377.516), the two lines disappear when the tube uses clamped taps. The same ledger reports remaining seams in **Now You're Stalking** (`E22_KF_1`, node 27) without a location. I inspected the sampled views and traced the visible joins described below. [Ledger](../playtest/2026-11-19-playtest-19.md) · [Before and after at the hatch](../../build/escape-seams-001/E11-spawn-detail.png) · [Before and after across the wood and steel](../../build/escape-seams-001/E11-band-detail.png)

| View | Cause and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Escape to Victory, vertical line behind the hatch | Inner-shell tile edge at column 64, between buckets 7 and 8. At pixel (324,112), `u=0.000861` and the first bilinear tap is column −1. Wrapping mixed bitmap columns 127 and 0. Clamping selects the local edge and changes RGB (72,65,43) to (66,60,40) at the same depth. |
| Escape to Victory, horizontal line under the hatch | Outer-shell tile edge at row 32, between bands 1 and 2. At the wood pixel (238,312) and steel pixel (391,313), `v=0.997665` and `0.999758` respectively. Both sampled rows 127 and 0 of their own tile under wrap. Clamping removes the contrasting taps along the row at every sampled yaw. |
| Now You're Stalking, sampled bark and steel joins | At yaw 96/256, sharp shading near inner column 224 is visible in the decoded bark tiles. Both quads in the sampled band share vertex indices 785 and 786. At yaw 160/256 the steel line crosses the interior of one quad and matches texel rows 95–96 in the installed tile. The H2 arrow and rail samples at yaw 80/256 still match the accepted clamp result. I could not reproduce a further render-induced seam in the sampled views or tie either artwork join to the unspecified J1 location. |

All 44 instrumented tube depth buffers were byte-identical before and after. The stored key lookup resolved every vertex. On both levels the maximum recorded separation at matched same-shell edge endpoints, including T-junctions, was below `8e-15` world units. These measurements and the sample UVs identify texture bleed on the two Escape to Victory lines. **Production path.** `TubeScene::build` selects the existing clamp mode for the `E11_KF_1` INN and OUT tiles, alongside the existing `E22_KF_1` tiles. From `runApplicationLeg → scheduleSceneFrame → TubeScene::render → drawRouteQuad → Raster::triangle`, the address mode selects RGB taps after coverage and depth acceptance. Simulation, picking and actor occlusion continue to use their existing inputs and depth writes. Scoping clamp to the demonstrated asset families is provisional. The retained global clamp attempt changed the `MM_CLIMB_002` comparison frames. [Changed source](../../src/app/TubeScene.cpp#L154) · [Retained comparison result](stalking-seams-001.md)

**Original static evidence.** Ghidra 12.1.4 opened the registered x86 `Lemmings Revolution/Lemmings Revolution.exe`, SHA-256 `32675bdb49866e76502713251fdfd2b09078c2481c72ca22ff68ebf4b3737a2f`. The xrefs at `0x43A6BB` and `0x43A908` in walker `0x43A4EC` bracket the four terrain calls at `0x43A754`, `0x43A7F4`, `0x43A87C` and `0x43A8FA`. The instructions push 1 at `0x43A6B4` and 0 at `0x43A901`. Setter `0x470FAF` selects CLAMP 3 or WRAP 1 at `0x470FB8..0x470FD8` for stage 0 address state `0x0C`. The cell mesh at `0x463410` uses the shared key lookup `0x43A31D`. This verifies the requested address state and the mesh construction independently of clone observations. **Checks.** The Windows build and Emscripten browser build each produced 44 framebuffers per level at 640×480 including scenery-on and scene-off pairs. They cover the hatch entry and settled cameras, 16 rotation views at 22.5° intervals and four tilted views. Entry yaw is 191/256 for node 28 and 240/256 for node 27, with eye distance 727.516 and pitch −0.0313577 turns. The altered pitches are −0.046875 and −0.015625 turns. Each PNG has a camera JSON and command log beside it. I saw no new gaps at the sampled poses. The 44 Stalking desktop frames have identical RGB before and after. Desktop and Wasm agree on the two Escape to Victory seam views. Both levels have a small RGB difference at yaw 96/256 between platforms. In the spot checks `MM_CLIMB_002` matched and `MM_DIG_000` and `E19_KF_2` differed only at flame sprites.
