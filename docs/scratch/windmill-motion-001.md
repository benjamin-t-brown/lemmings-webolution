# windmill-motion-001

**Needs review.** Playtest 25 Q6 reported static windmill blades in **Escape to victory** (`E11_KF_1`, map node 28). The incoming clone rendered the child's serialized basis. Its scenery clock advanced water UVs and sprites, while the clip's rotation records were skipped by the LVL reader. [Playtest 25](../playtest/2026-11-21-playtest-25.md)

**Installed data and static trace.** The `myanim` clip in the secondary `Spooky_Forest` holder starts at LVL offset `0xC030A`. Its third array contains a 16-byte record at `0xC5156` with preorder target `6`, axis `2`, and rate `0.2` turns per second. Target 6 is `windmill_roll_0.2_trans` at `0xB6ED3`, the child of `cube1_trans`. The reference EXE is SHA-256 `32675bdb49866e76502713251fdfd2b09078c2481c72ca22ff68ebf4b3737a2f`. The clip reader `0x476277` calls `0x477510 → 0x47CDE7` to read the spin array. The resolver `0x47645E` builds the preorder table through `0x46BAFE` and binds the track through `0x476325 → 0x47CE27`. The world update at `0x442445 → 0x437BDE → 0x4764C4` wraps clip zero and calls `0x476EE0` at `0x476A6D` with absolute clip time. Axis 2 selects `0x481F3C`, which replaces the local Z basis and preserves translation. Ghidra pseudocode and the instruction reads agree on these roles. [Addresses, instructions and pseudocode](../../build/windmill-motion-001/static/trace.md) · [Raw installed records](../../build/windmill-motion-001/installed-records.tsv)

The blades turn about their local Z axis through the stored hub `(0.0008709935, 2.3293170929, -0.8744073510)` in the body's coordinates. One turn takes five seconds. The authored phase starts at zero and wraps after `16.66666603088379` seconds, so the clip can reset the blade angle at that boundary. The paused original dispatch at `0x45DD76` supplies zero elapsed time. The clone applies its existing capped presentation clock, with a frozen phase during pause and one advance per displayed frame during fast-forward. Original playback appearance remains unobserved.

**Production path.** The [LVL reader](../../decomp-lemmings/src/object_base_prefix.cpp) exposes the [spin records](../../decomp-lemmings/include/lemmings/object_base_prefix.hpp). `BackdropScene::build → bindSecondaryAnimation` binds them to the secondary tree. The existing app call to `advanceScenery → advanceSecondaryAnimation` samples the clip and updates the blade matrix used by `renderStage → buildSceneMeshSource`. The [scene implementation](../../src/app/BackdropScene.cpp) and [state](../../src/app/BackdropScene.h) use the same rotation setter as placed gadgets.
