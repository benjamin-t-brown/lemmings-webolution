# Playtest 3, 2026-11-13

Human play by the user on the release build of commit 86484b0 via scripts/Play-Clone.ps1.

Confirmed fixed by a person:
- P7: walking is much smoother. Clicking seems fine; precision not yet stressed.
- P17: fast-forward on and off works as expected.
- P9: the hatch opens (see R1, R2 for remaining defects).
- P11: the balloon leaves (see R4).

Not fixed or newly reported:
- R1: lemmings come out of the hatch too soon; they should appear after the "let's go" text disappears.
- R2: the hatch door animation seems too fast.
- R3 (P8 not fixed): cylinder occlusion is wrong in both directions. Lemmings are drawn behind side
  walls that should hide them, and pop out of view where they should be visible. Easiest to see on
  climbers, which are closest to the wall they climb. The user suspects the lack of a per-pixel
  depth test.
- R4: the balloon flies straight up; in the original it spirals out slowly.
- R5: lemmings do not highlight when hovered with the cursor.
- R6: hovering the hatch does not highlight it or show how many lemmings are left.
- R7: the skill panel on the left does not animate a job icon when hovered or active.
- R8: on a level where lemmings are probably meant to be upside down, they are drawn clipping
  through the ground or standing on nothing, and never appear upside down. Level not named
  (map hover names were not yet available in this build).
