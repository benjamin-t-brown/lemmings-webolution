## Defects
- T1: after Try again the level music starts at once; in the original it starts about 3.3 s after
  the click and the lemmings appear about 4.3 s after it (the clone's spawn time seems right).
  Static reading: loader 0x4432C1 only loads the track (0x498DE8 mode 1 at 0x444392, which calls
  0x49905A first and fills the DirectSound buffer without playing it). The per-frame playing update
  0x45CC09 counts the "Let's Go!" title clock (+0x25FF8, seeded by 0x45C9AB: 3.5 after map entry,
  1.5 without the intro, which covers Retry) down by frame time while not paused (+0x572C == 0);
  when it drops below -1.0 it clears +0x26024 and calls 0x498D04, which rewinds the buffer (vtable
  +0x3C, position 0), starts looping playback (vtable +0x30, flags 1) and applies the volume
  (0x498993). So music starts when the title cue expires: 2.5 s of unpaused play after Retry, 4.5 s
  after map entry. The clone calls playLevelMusic before level presentation (src/app/main.cpp).
  Other callers of 0x498D04: 0x450B26, 0x45CFF9, 0x486BBA. Queued as level-music-start-001.
- T2: holding a mouse button on a hatch changes the release rate far slower than the original,
  where holding right click from 1 reaches 99 in about 2 s. Static reading: the per-frame
  camera and input update 0x4532E2 (frame time argument) calls 0x425DE3 with rate + 1 while
  0x455DD0 (0x455DF0, held state) holds and rate - 1 while 0x455D90 (0x455DB0) holds, for the
  selected hatch +0x1894, on every update with no delay or repeat timer: one step per frame. The
  clone (RuntimeReleaseRateControl::advance) steps on press, waits 0.4 s, then steps every 0.1 s,
  about 10 s from 1 to 99. Queued as rate-hold-speed-001.
