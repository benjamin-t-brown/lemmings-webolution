Distance-to-wall was the cause; row range, bite phase and the picker were not (hover matched the target at the click, `hover=0`).

## 2. What the original does (static reading, not measured)
Addresses in the registered EXE. Reading is of Ghidra pseudocode cross-checked against the constructor's instructions.
* Constructor `0x0040BBA0` (vtable `0x004B06AC`): `[+0x41]=0` (removed-anything flag), `[+0x44]=0` (cycle counter). It registers 24 percent records at 4% steps: 4%/8%, 32-48%, 72-88% -> `0x0040C7F6`; 12/16/60/64% -> bite `0x0040C357` with args 0/1/3/2 (matches the measured bite phases already in `Rules`); 92% -> `0x0040C6E8`.
* Cell helper `0x0040C0AF`: refuses cells with bit 0x2, the facing's directional bit (4, or 8 when `[+0x40]==-1`) or 0x800 (blocker field), plays Scrape and returns 1; otherwise a nonzero cell sets `[+0x41]=1`, is zeroed, and (args 0 and 3) spawns debris and the Mine cue; returns 0.
* Bite `0x0040C357`: any refusal -> `FUN_0042F8B9(lemming)` (leave the state) and return 1. Also, after the arg-0 bite: `if ([+0x41]==0 && arg==0 && [+0x44]==2) FUN_0042F8B9`.
* End-of-cycle `0x0040C6E8`: `[+0x48] = trunc([+0x48] + facing)` and the lemming's X follows (one column per cycle). `if ([+0x44]==2 && [+0x41]==0) FUN_0042F8B9`; else if `FUN_0042E913` (ground probe, inference) is false, construct the fall state `FUN_00415FA0`; else `if ([+0x44]<2) ++[+0x44]; [+0x41]=0` and keep bashing.
* So: a new Basher does not end at its first empty bite. The first two cycles advance a column each through open air; from the third cycle a cycle that removed nothing ends the bash, with no turn. It ends when it runs out of wall, on steel or a refusal, or off the ground.

## 3. Change (production)
* `src/sim/World.h`: `Lemming::bashCycles`; `Rules::basherGraceCycles = 2` with the evidence above and the provisional differences.
* `src/sim/World.cpp` `World::stepBasher`: an empty leading column ends the bash only when `bashCycles >= basherGraceCycles`; otherwise it still advances one column and counts the cycle; the cleared box is only removed when something solid is there; after the advance, no ground under the lemming -> Faller. Steel still turns the lemming into a Walker. `World::assign` resets `bashCycles` for a Basher.
* Production call path: SDL click -> `main.cpp` (`runtimeSkillPanelCommand`, `skillSelection.consumePanelClick`, `hoveredActor`) -> `World::assign(Skill::Basher)` -> per tick `World::step` -> `stepBasher`.
* Effect on a player: a lemming up to three cells from a tall wall (cell `x+dir*3` is the first solid one) now tunnels it; at four or more cells the Basher swings twice, advances two columns and ends as a Walker without turning and without removing terrain. Against a wall one tick from turning round the behaviour is unchanged.

Provisional (to be settled by an original observation of a Basher assigned two or three cells short of a wall, and one beside an overhang):
* The clone tests all four rows of the leading column at once on the cycle's bite tick; the original's 12% record sees only the feet row for the stop test. A wall that starts above the feet row is treated as wall by the clone.
* The clone advances at the bite tick, the original at 92% of the cycle.
* `FUN_0042E913` as the ground probe is an inference (it is cited that way in `RuntimePick.cpp`); the clone's ground test is `solidAt(x, y+1)`.

Not changed: Miner and Digger (the same defect was not demonstrated for them; Miner has its own, separate first-bite code path and Digger refuses/falls on its own rules), timing, release rate, other skills, steel handling.
