**Original static evidence.** Ghidra pseudocode cross-checked against instructions in the registered `Lemmings Revolution.exe` (SHA-256 `32675bdb49866e76502713251fdfd2b09078c2481c72ca22ff68ebf4b3737a2f`) shows that miner helper `0x4188F6` resets its per-subpass clear flag at `0x4189A2` and sets it at `0x418B22` just before clearing a cell at `0x418B29`. When the flag is zero and the subpass argument is `-1`, `0x418D57..0x418D62` enters the skill exit. With the separate protected-cell refusal flag clear, `0x418D7F` jumps to the selector call at `0x419008`. Selector `0x42F8B9` constructs the walking state via `0x41BD60` at `0x42F8F4` and attaches it at `0x42F91F`. The empty-subpass exit uses the clear flag, the subpass argument and the refusal flag alone.
