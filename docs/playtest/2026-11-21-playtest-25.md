## Defects
- Q1: clicking or right-clicking a hatch to change the release rate gets interrupted, and the click
  has to be repeated. Source trace: while a hold is active, main.cpp re-resolves the hatch under the
  pointer each frame through hatchAtPointer, which gives a hovered lemming precedence;
  RuntimeReleaseRateControl::advance (src/app/RuntimeSkillPanel.cpp:54) ends the hold as soon as the
  result differs from the held hatch, so a lemming dropping out of the hatch under the cursor cancels
  it. The same precedence can make the initial press miss the hatch. Queued as hatch-rate-hold-001.
- Q5: on Just Climb (MM_CLIMB_002, node 0) the water does not move, neither the pool at the base nor
  the stream from the pipe. Queued as water-motion-001.
- Q6: on Escape to victory (E11_KF_1, node 28) the background windmill's blades do not turn. The
  clone draws the serialized pose of child node windmill_roll_0.2_trans only
  (docs/scratch/scene-nested-impl-001.md). Queued as windmill-motion-001.
- Q11: in the original, the plank a builder pulls from the sack rotates into place as well as
  scaling; the clone only scales and moves it. builder-brick-anim-001 traced two rotations at 4105B0
  and left them out as provisional. Queued as builder-brick-rotate-001.
