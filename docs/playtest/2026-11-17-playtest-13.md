## Asked and answered
- On One Way Ticket, lemmings walking up the earth slope turned at stairs built from the slope
  in the opposite direction, while climbers got over. The lead traced attempt 3
  (MM_ALL_8_001-1794973381801119100-3.replay): the builder started at x 242 on top of a two-cell
  earth step, so its first brick capped that step and made it three high. Walkers step up two
  and turn at three (measured live in the original, World.h maxStepUp), and a brick is plain
  solid (measured live, 72 of 72 cells). The user then confirmed this for themselves and won.
  Not a defect.

## Reported by the user
- C1: pressing Space to zoom in does not zoom toward the cursor; where it lands seems random.
  Source trace: CameraController::update zooms on its own cursor, which is integrated from raw
  relative mouse motion at 2.0x / 2.4x and clamped, not on the visible pointer that picking and
  the panel use. The target is also coarse: elevation from the cursor row and at most a
  30-unit yaw nudge.
- C2: dragging with the right mouse button turns the cylinder the wrong way. The user chose:
  reverse left/right only; up/down stays. An option to choose either direction is post-1.0.
- C3: fast-forward should be held, not toggled: on while the button is held, off on release.
- C4: on One Way Ticket, early on while lemmings walk back and forth near the reverse-bash zone,
  lemmings above a certain height are coloured correctly and darker below it. Source trace: the
  clone indexes the level's actor light map with the lemming's 3D model position; the original
  uses its terrain X and Y (0x403F40 with actor+0x08/+0x10). One Way Ticket's map has shadow
  bands under ledges, so the wrong index shows; MM_CLIMB_002, where this was checked, is all one
  value. The miner axe copies the same lookup.
- C5: One Way Ticket's background is clearly wrong: large black surfaces where the original shows
  a green water plane under a dark blue sky. Screenshots by the user at level start:
  reference/playtest-13/one_way_ticket_orig.jpg (original) and one_way_ticket_web.jpg (clone).
  The clone reports no missing textures or null materials, so placement or render state is the
  suspect, not missing assets.
