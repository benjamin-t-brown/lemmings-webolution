## Reported by the user
General:
- A1: lemmings which explode should not destroy metal surfaces.
- A2: lemmings who nuke should play their explosion sound.
- A3: the nuke timer should be in big text that covers the centre of the screen.
- A4: some occlusion clipping remains when a lemming is falling close to a surface. The user sees
  this in the original too and suggests it as a later improvement. Deferred: docs/POST_1_0.md.
- A5: performance: still seeing some issues; it should run at 60+ fps on the user's hardware.
- A6: bash, mine and dig particles are 2D rectangles; they should be 3D rotating particles.

Kriss Kross (node 19):
- A7: water lemmings should not exit the level in the normal balloon, and vice versa.

Sergeant Bash (node 20):
- A8: miners should play sounds like bashers do.
- A9: the basher works slightly differently in the real game. Every punch plays a bash sound and
  throws particles, but it takes two hits to remove a piece of wall: the piece is split in two,
  the bottom part removed first, then the top part.

The Crowded House (node 21):
- A10: digging on snow tilts adjacent terrain towards the dig. It should not tilt metal terrain.
- A11: the dig should play a sound.

Not contradicted, so taken as acceptable for now: playtest 10 Z1-Z7 (round log, actor order,
Let's Go text, nuke Oh No, Abyss fall, simultaneous hatches, crab under a full tank).
