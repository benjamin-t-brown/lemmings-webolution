# Playtest 29 U2 · Teleport partner hover

On **The Lone Ranger** (`MM_TELE_003`, node 38), portals could brighten as switch receivers and hovering them directly produced no highlight. `BackdropScene::gadgetPresentation` records each base and its outgoing partner from `resolveTeleportPartners`. `runApplicationLeg → renderFrame` tracks fragment ownership, and `materialize → BackdropScene::drawSwitchHover` applies saturating RGB addition to the selected roots. HUD plates, icons and counters suppress portal hover before composition. Cursor policy, skill assignment and simulation keep their existing paths. [Playtest 29 U2](../playtest/2026-11-22-playtest-29.md#requests) · [Switch highlight](switch-hover-highlight-002.md)

On **Green with Envy** (`VH12_KF1`, node 88), the first implementation could pick a pad by its bounding rectangle while the pointer was on a visible portal fragment. `drawSwitchHover` now checks the rendered owner at the pointer first, including idle ring fragments that leave scene depth intact. For other pixels it checks the existing lever and pad bounds in their original order, then falls back to portal bounds. Switch receiver lookup and the occupancy-sensor callback follow the existing path. [Revision diff](../../build/teleport-hover-partner-001/priority/revision.diff)

The highlight follows one outgoing link. Either end of a reciprocal pair brightens both bases. A one-way link brightens its departure base and destination, and an unlinked pad brightens only its own base. The bases include their idle rings. The separate `teleport-fx-0` and `teleport-fx-1` roots provide transient departure and arrival shafts, so they keep their original clone brightness. This is provisional clone feedback. The inspected original paths establish linking and draw roles. Original portal hover remains unverified.
