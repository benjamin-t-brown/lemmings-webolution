# Native OpenGL scenery passes

The November 24 OpenGL request moved these passes ahead of playable v1. In `runApplicationLeg`, `prepareLevelScene` records the admitted draws, including the secondary mesh, billboard and sprite dispatch order. The sprite latch runs in that preparation step. `SoftwareLevelRaster` renders the complete frame for the window. `--compare-opaque DIR` also sends the same prepared records through `OpaqueSceneComparison` to the native GL context. The GPU consumes the projected winding, depth, RHW, UVs, mirror and live fluid vertices and colours from those records. [Playtest request](../playtest/2026-11-24-opengl-request.md) · [Application](../../src/app/main.cpp) · [Backdrop preparation](../../src/app/BackdropScene.cpp) · [Comparison](../../src/app/OpaqueSceneComparison.cpp)

The pipe pass writes accepted fragments to an auxiliary depth attachment. The fluid pass copies scene depth into independent pre-water and post-water targets. Each blended triangle samples a copy of the preceding destination and rounds the weighted sum once. The four material selectors preserve replacement, source-alpha interpolation, one-plus-one, and source-alpha addition. This removed two-byte secondary errors that occurred with fixed-function blending at pixels with the same triangle owner. Keyed sprites retain the strict alpha reference, and caret billboards retain their separate alpha policy. The sky shader reads the completed colour and depth and writes to a separate framebuffer, then copies its colour back. All targets are checked for completeness.
