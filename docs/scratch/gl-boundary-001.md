[Playtest request](../playtest/2026-11-24-opengl-request.md) · [Renderer plan](../OPENGL_PLAN.md)

`runApplicationLeg → prepareScene → prepareLevelScene` syncs presentation state and records projected draws with leg-owned textures. `PreparedLevelScene::passes` places primary, legacy, tube, gadget effects, pipes, fluid, secondary, counter gates, optional sky and drowning-depth completion, explosion marks, actors, particles, and late teleport effects. The diagnostic sequence records the two tube ranges with the early actor pass between them. `renderLevelScene → SoftwareLevelRaster::draw` walks that list and submits through `Raster::triangle` and `rasterizeSceneTriangle`. The consumer owns its caches and depth planes. Terrain footprint data is snapshotted by revision, and the branch-1 sprite latch runs during preparation. [Application](../../src/app/main.cpp#L772) · [Pass order and software dispatch](../../src/app/PreparedLevelFrame.h#L16) · [Backdrop preparation](../../src/app/BackdropScene.cpp#L1371) · [Tube preparation](../../src/app/TubeScene.cpp#L1649)

After scene rasterization, `ensureDisplayedScene` holds the camera, placed actor boxes, and visible hatch bounds through input dispatch. Skill assignment and hatch input use those boxes. Zoom asks the token for finite actor depth first, then scene depth. `PreparedLevelLayers` preserves the order of the resolved hover, panel, HUD, menu, cue, and cursor draws. Each base, selected, and fast-forward plate stores its texture and logical destination rectangle. The entry cue has a one-shot voice request at preparation.
