## Outcome and autonomy

**C3 only:** deliver one actual controller/minimal-observer integration and, if it
passes the readiness conditions below, make one bounded native observation. This
is an observation-feasibility pass, not full-frame equivalence or renderer repair.

The facilitator selects, splits, dispatches, repairs, verifies and lands the work
within this scope without returning for approval at each offline stage. Once readiness
is independently established, it may proceed to the single live stage without another
user turn. A safety/permission change still requires approval. No synthetic replacement
controller, repeated design-only preflight, full logger or comparator-polishing project.

## Offline integration and mandatory readiness conditions

1. **One canonical controller path.** Repair the pinned operational controller's actual
   serialization/integration defects, preserving its guards and cleanup. Keep historical
   sources unchanged; make an auditable successor with an exact diff and hashes. Its
   dry-run and authorized execution must share the actual prelaunch path. Do not replace
   `main()` with a simplified checker or delete the operational suffix and call that
   integration proof. Dry-run stops before backup/install writes, start reservation,
   game creation or attach; native execution requires explicit authorization for the
   verified artifact, never an accidentally armed default.
2. **Executable minimal observer.** Implement the bounded design in
   [C3aw](scratch/phase-c3aw-site-preflight.md), preserving its site/route and collection
   limits, raw bytes, attribution limits, unknowns, omission accounting and restoration
   requirements. Pin the actual implementation and dependencies, not merely its design.
   Offline tests exercise the real observer logic where possible; synthetic success does
   not establish native relocation, reachability or non-interference.
3. **Independent integrated readiness check.** The facilitator reviews the exact
   controller, observer, bindings and cleanup together; repeats the actual session1
   prelaunch dry-run; checks failure controls and exact owned-resource release; and
   records the approved artifact identities and all runtime-only unknowns. Relevant
   code/dependency changes invalidate readiness until rechecked. A successful standalone
   guard or test double is insufficient. No start if any safety prerequisite is unresolved.

These conditions resolve the specific shortfall in
[C3ax](scratch/phase-c3ax-controller-preflight.md). Reuse AW/AX evidence; do not restart
their investigations or promote partial packets. The facilitator owns briefs and checks.
