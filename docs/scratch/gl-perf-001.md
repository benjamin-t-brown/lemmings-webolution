# gl-perf-001

The November 24 playtest reported 18.18 ms mean native GL frames in Love Train Boogie (`M01_KF_1`) against 4.95 ms in software. The retained revision removed synchronous frame readbacks. This revision reuses stationary scenery and linked programs. On the RX 9070 XT at 144 Hz, native GL beat software on mean and p95 active frame cost in all 108 workloads. Forty workloads met the 2 ms p95 target, down from 47 in the preceding matrix. Every GL cold first present was slower than software, and swap stalls remain. ## Production path

- `runApplicationLeg` passes the SDL presenter's context-owned program cache to `createOpaqueGpu`. Level legs keep independent geometry, textures and framebuffers. Native levels link the sky fill only if they draw that pass. On Just Climb, the first visit's scene setup took 167.14 ms after the window was ready and Retry took 0.42 ms with the same context. [App dispatch](../../src/app/main.cpp) · [Cache](../../src/app/NativeGlApi.h) · [Retry log](../../build/gl-perf-001/presentation-final-checks/menu-map/run.log)
- `OpaqueSceneComparison::draw → SceneGpu::draw` reuses the primary pass's colour, encoded world depth and raster depth when the prepared geometry, viewport and depth interval match. It leaves per-frame passes in order, batches compatible adjacent draws, and disconnects unused outputs from the native draw buffers. The ordinary path keeps the reduction targets needed by drowning effects. A trial that removed them failed Kriss Kross at frame 47, and the completed production matrix exercised that route with the resources restored. [Scene implementation](../../src/app/NativeOpaqueComparison.cpp) · [Trial log](../../build/gl-perf-001/presentation-primary-E19_KF_2-rotate.log)
- `NativeGlPresenter` sets swap interval to 0. It polls timer availability before using a prior result and drops pending samples on level exit without reading them. The final Retry profile has no sample from the preceding leg on its frame 0. Ordinary native input uses the prepared displayed frame. Explicit shot and comparison consumers can request readback. [Presenter](../../src/app/NativeGlPresenter.cpp) · [Retry CSV](../../build/gl-perf-001/presentation-final-checks/menu-map/frames.csv)

## Native measurements

Registered GCC 16.1.0 Release, OpenGL 3.3 core, Radeon driver 26.10.1.261109, 144 Hz actual refresh and SDL Direct3D for software with vsync off. Each of Just Climb, Sergeant Bash, Kriss Kross, The Crowded House, One Way Ticket and Love Train Boogie ran at rest, with eight physical drag pixels per frame, and with fast-forward held. Three warm samples per workload and backend covered drawable scales 1×, 2× and 3× with and without `--pointer 320,240`. The harness seeded 180 ticks and sampled frames 30–229 of 240 with a fixed 1/60 s budget and audio off. The 648-run inventory reports mean, median, p95, p99, max, GPU samples, draws, uploaded bytes and CPU buckets. The matrix was collected before the final level-exit timer cleanup. Final executable spot runs and the Retry probe checked that cleanup. The median stationary workload fell from 354 to 280 GL draws per frame. Stationary and fast-forward median mean costs fell from 2.03 to 1.97 ms and 2.13 to 2.04 ms. The rotating median mean rose from 2.23 to 2.28 ms. GL p95 ranged from 1.27 to 4.07 ms. All 64,800 expected GPU samples arrived and ordinary GL readback time was zero in each of 324 runs. Draw counts cover draw submissions. GPU time also covers framebuffer blits. [Prior pooled results](../../build/gl-perf-001/revision-native-144hz/summary.tsv) · [Current pooled results](../../build/gl-perf-001/presentation-native-144hz/summary.tsv)

The mean CPU time inside swap ranged from 0.60 to 2.03 ms across runs. In Love Train Boogie at rest at 2× with the pointer, swap averaged 1.71 ms, preparation 0.62 ms, scene submission 0.234 ms, composition 0.014 ms, window copy 0.001 ms, query polling 0.003 ms and other work 0.036 ms. The worst measured frame was Sergeant Bash holding fast-forward at 2× with the pointer, where frame 69 spent 75.97 of 76.39 ms in swap. The previous build stalled on the same frame. With sound enabled on Love Train Boogie, the app observer measured 2.68 ms mean and 3.97 ms p95 GL intervals over 199 readable samples. Display scanout cadence and the driver's internal cause of the swap spikes remain unverified.
