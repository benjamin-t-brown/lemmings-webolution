## Static evidence and production change

REA/Ghidra 12.1.4 inspected the registered x86 `Lemmings Revolution.exe`, SHA-256 `32675bdb49866e76502713251fdfd2b09078c2481c72ca22ff68ebf4b3737a2f`. The constructor references paint at `0x40F9B2` and advance at `0x40FA10`. Paint takes the biased feet at `0x410141` and ORs bit `1` into the two facing grid cells at `0x4101EB..0x4101FE`. Advance applies the signed rise at `0x40FBF6..0x40FC29`. Reversal at `0x42F5ED..0x42F65D` negates the rise sign and swaps the Y biases for feet and head.

The forward-foot check at `0x40FE77..0x40FE7F` and the forward-head check at `0x410032..0x4100A6` each test the full word returned by `0x40DE60`. A nonzero foot cell turns before brick decrement. With bricks remaining after an advance, a nonzero head cell turns after decrement. The same comparisons apply in both gravity directions. Water and lava write `0x200` and `0x2000` during receiver load and height updates (`0x42AF32..0x42AFC4`, `0x42B381..0x42B4CC`). The constructor at `0x428A47` also writes `0x4000` for acid. The set painter at `0x4311D0` truncates receiver bounds and excludes the upper row. Receiver participation in the separate object-overlap check at `0x40FE07` and retail behavior after an acid receiver drains remain unverified. REA closed without a snapshot. [Pseudocode, xrefs and instructions](../../build/inverted-builder-fluid-001/rea-static.json)

The frontend and replay assign Builder through `World::assign`. `RuntimeSimState::advanceElapsed` reaches `World::updateSegment → stepElapsed → step → stepBuilder`. Both Builder probes now treat a cell within the live, rounded receiver bounds as an obstruction. Walkers and fallers continue through the existing fluid-hazard logic. The clone uses live height as a provisional substitute for the original grid word’s fluid bits. A mask-only prototype turned an upright builder at `(112,44)` on **Wheelbarrows of Doom** (`M08_MG_1`, node 32) at step 21384 because an acid bit persisted at receiver height zero. That prototype diverged at step 22669 when the expected first rescue was missing. The builder crosses the dry basin in the final build and the recording wins again.
